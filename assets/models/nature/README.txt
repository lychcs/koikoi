Nature models imported by tools/import_nature_models.py
=======================================================

Source: "Ultimate Stylized Nature - May 2022" by Quaternius
        https://quaternius.com/
License: CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain dedication)

Only the low-poly models used by the overworld are copied here. The source
pack's very large 2048px bark sheets are downsampled to 512px and the foliage
textures are recolored onto the game's Mokuhanga palette. The authored MTL
files add the missing map_Kd links from the source pack.

Models: MapleTree_4, PineTree_1, PineTree_3, Rock_1, Rock_3,
        Bush_Small, Bush_Large, Grass_Small, Grass_Large_Extruded,
        Flower_1_Clump
